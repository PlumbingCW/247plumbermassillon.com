How a tankless water heater works

When a hot tap opens, flow sensors fire a burner or heating element and heat the water as it passes through a heat exchanger. There is no tank to heat and hold, so standby loss is low and the supply does not run out until you exceed the unit's flow limit.

Tankless units fit homes with high or irregular demand, and they free floor space. They are not right for every house, because the gas line, the vent and the water quality all affect how well they run.

Water treatmentA softener or scale filter protects the heat exchanger.
PermitsGas appliance work may need a permit.

Tankless units are not an insurance matter unless a failure caused damage. Keep your install records and flush schedule.

Tankless in Massillon: water quality matters

Massillon's public water comes mostly from Aqua Ohio's Massillon Division wells in the Tuscarawas Valley. We do not quote a hardness number here because it changes. Ask the utility or test your tap. If the water is hard, scale builds up in a tankless heat exchanger faster than in a tank, so a softener or filter and an annual flush protect your investment.

A tankless unit that shows an error code or runs cold often needs a flush, a vent check or a new sensor. We service units we did not install, and we explain the repair before we start.

Running a tankless heater well

Tankless units reward a little attention. Keep the area around the unit clear, check that the vent terminal is free of snow and debris, and listen for new noises. A unit that cycles on and off at low flow may need a different setting, and a unit that cuts out when two showers run may be undersized.

Flush the heat exchanger on the schedule your plumber suggests, and keep the service records. If the water gets cold at one tap and stays hot at others, the fault may be a mixing valve or a crossed line, and a short visit sorts it out.

A tankless install follows the maker's instructions for gas sizing, venting and condensate, and the standards in the Ohio Plumbing Code, OAC Chapter 4101:3.
