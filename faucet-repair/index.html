A faucet that will not stop leaking

If water sprays or runs and will not stop, close the shutoff valves under the sink. Turn them clockwise until snug. If there are none, close the main valve for the house. This limits damage while you wait.

A slow drip is less urgent but not harmless. A faucet dripping once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year, and the drip often hides a worn part that gets worse.

Drip from the spout

A worn cartridge, washer or seat lets water pass when the handle is closed.

Water at the handle

A failed O-ring or packing leaks around the stem.

Water under the sink

A supply line, a connection or the faucet body may be leaking.

Low flow

Mineral buildup in the aerator or the cartridge blocks the water.

Hard water and old faucets in Massillon

Water in Massillon comes largely from Aqua Ohio's wells in the Tuscarawas Valley aquifer. Ask the utility for the current hardness figure, because minerals build up in aerators and cartridges wherever the water is hard. Spotty glass and white crust on the faucet are signs.

Many older Massillon kitchens and baths still have the original shutoff valves, which seize when you try to use them. We replace failed stops during a repair so the next leak can be isolated at the fixture, and we can suggest a water softener or filter if scale is the cause.

Replacement faucets and supply stops should meet the fixture standards in the Ohio Plumbing Code, OAC Chapter 4101:3, and we follow the maker's instructions for each model.
