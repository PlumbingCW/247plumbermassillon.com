What every visit includes

Every visit starts with a plain description of the problem and a clear price before work starts. We explain what we found, what the options are and what each costs. We do not push work you do not need.

Our crews serve Massillon and nearby towns, including Perry Township, Jackson Township, Navarre, Canal Fulton and North Canton. Same-day appointments are offered when available. Pricing varies by job, and each service page lists the factors that move it.
