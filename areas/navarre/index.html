Basements near the Tuscarawas River

Homes near a river can sit above shallow groundwater. In wet seasons, water can rise through the floor or the wall joint. A sump pump with a battery backup is standard protection, and we install and repair them.

Sewage coming up a floor drain during a storm is a separate issue. It may be the line or the system's capacity. A visit for sewer line cleaning and a camera look shows which one you have.

What to tell us when you call from Navarre

A short call gets the right plumber to the door with the right parts. Tell us your street, whether you are inside the village limits, and where the water comes from. If you have a private well, mention it, because a well pump or a pressure tank changes the plan. If you are on village water and sewer, tell us what you see at the drain or the fixture.

Then tell us what you have already done. If you shut off the main, say where it is. If you found a leak but not the source, describe the stain and when it started. Those details let us book the next appointment with a clear idea of the job, and you hear a clear price before work starts.

Small villages like Navarre often have homes of very different ages on the same street, from older frame houses to newer builds. A house from the early twentieth century may have galvanized supply lines, while a newer one has PEX. We bring the fittings for both and confirm on arrival.
