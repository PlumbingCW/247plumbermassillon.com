A broken water service line

The service line is the pipe that carries water from the utility's main, through the meter, to your house. If it breaks, water either surfaces in the yard or flows underground where you cannot see it. A spongy patch of lawn, a sudden drop in pressure or a meter that spins with the house shut off are the common signs.

Close the valve on the house side of the meter if you can reach it. If the break is before the meter, call the utility as well as us, because the utility controls the shutoff at the main.

Homeowner's insurance often excludes underground line repair unless a service line endorsement is in place. Check your policy.

Massillon service lines: lead and old pipe

Aqua Ohio's Massillon Division serves most Massillon addresses, and the utility reports its 2024 lead and copper sampling found 33 samples with none over the action level. The utility also maintains a service line material inventory, which you can check through its lead map. If your service line is old and unlined, replacing it during a repair is worth discussing.

In Perry Township both the City of Canton and Aqua Ohio serve water, and the name on your bill tells you which one serves you. That matters because the utility's shutoff and meter rules differ. Tell us your utility when you call and we plan the repair around it.

Water service piping follows the material and installation standards in the Ohio Plumbing Code, OAC Chapter 4101:3, and your utility sets its own rules at the meter.
