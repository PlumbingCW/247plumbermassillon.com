Checking any plumber's license

You are welcome to check the license of any plumber who comes to your home, including ours. In Ohio, plumbing contractors are licensed under the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board, which was established under Chapter 4740 of the Ohio Revised Code. The board can confirm whether a contractor is licensed.

The Massillon Building Department, at One James Duncan Plaza, registers plumbing contractors who work in the city each year and issues permits where they are required. Ask the department about any project before you hire.

Why we take calls by phone only

Plumbing problems are physical and urgent, and they are easier to sort out in a conversation than in a form. When you call, we can ask follow-up questions in real time. Is water still running? Do you know where the main valve is? Is the water near anything electrical? Those answers change what we tell you to do in the next five minutes.

A phone call also means you never wonder whether anyone saw your request. Someone picks up, hears the problem and books the visit. If you are away from home and a tenant or a neighbor sees the problem, they can call for you, and we explain what to shut off.

What a first visit looks like

The plumber arrives, listens to what you have seen and looks at the problem. We explain what we found in plain words, show you the part or the pipe when that helps, and give a clear price before we start. You decide whether to go ahead. If the job needs a permit, a second visit or a part that has to be ordered, we say so up front.

Afterward, we clean up, test the repair and tell you what to watch for. If your insurer needs a written description of what happened and what we did, ask and we provide one.

What we will not do

We will not tell you that every problem is an emergency, and we will not push work you do not need. If a repair can wait until morning, we say so and book it. If a part of the job is beyond plumbing, such as electrical work or foundation repair, we say that too and explain who to call.

If you rent, tell us who owns the property when you call. Many repairs need the owner's approval, and we can give you a written description of the problem to pass along, so nothing is delayed while you wait for an answer.

Our work follows the standards in the Ohio Plumbing Code, OAC Chapter 4101:3.
