When the boiler stops

A boiler heats water or makes steam and sends it through radiators, baseboards or in-floor loops. When it fails, the symptoms are no heat, uneven heat, banging pipes or water on the floor. Older homes in Massillon often have boilers installed decades ago and still working, which is a good reason to have them inspected.

If the boiler is leaking or you smell gas, shut it off and call. A gas smell means leave the building and call 911 from outside.

No heat

A failed circulator, a tripped limit, a dead igniter or no fuel can all cause it.

Banging or gurgling

Air in the system or low water pressure causes noise.

Water on the floor

A leaking relief valve, a weeping joint or a corroded section.

Pressure swings

A failed expansion tank or a bad fill valve changes pressure.

Boilers in older Massillon homes

Homes built before 1950 in Massillon, especially in the older neighborhoods near downtown, often have hydronic or steam heat. Many of these systems still run on original radiators and piping. The boiler is the part that wears out, and replacement is a larger decision than a repair.

If your boiler is over twenty years old and failing, ask for an honest repair or replace opinion. We give one, and you hear a clear price before work starts. Winter is the busiest time, so a fall tune-up is worth considering.

Preparing a boiler for winter

A boiler that sat idle all summer often fails on the first cold night. A fall check finds a weak pump, a stuck zone valve or a low pressure reading before the cold arrives. Look at the pressure gauge, check around the boiler for water or rust, and run each zone for a few minutes to confirm the radiators warm up.

Bleed air from radiators that stay cold at the top, and keep furniture and drapes from blocking baseboards. If the boiler is noisy, leaks or cycles on and off, book a visit before the heating season starts, because winter appointments fill quickly.

Boiler work follows the maker's instructions and the standards for relief valves, venting and fuel connections that apply to your fuel type.
